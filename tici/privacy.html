提詞 / PromptCam 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-10-05

中文

「提詞」不需註冊帳號，不含廣告、使用者追蹤、第三方分析 SDK 或第三方 AI 服務。

裝置上的資料：講稿、設定、錄影、縮圖及簡易裁切產生的影片保存在你的裝置或你主動選擇的位置。開發者沒有伺服器接收這些內容。

語音辨識：語音跟隨使用 Apple Speech，優先採用裝置端辨識；若目前裝置或語言不支援裝置端辨識，Apple 語音服務可能需要網路處理。App 不會把你的錄音或講稿傳送到開發者伺服器。

相機與麥克風：只在錄影及語音跟隨功能需要時使用。App 不會在背景偷偷錄音或錄影。

照片：只有在你主動選擇儲存影片時，才會要求新增到照片圖庫的權限。

影片裁切：簡易裁切在裝置上處理，裁切後會建立新的影片檔案並保留原始影片；影片不會上傳到開發者伺服器。

文件與分享：你匯入的文件只用來建立講稿；分享擴充只處理你主動分享給本 App 的內容。

刪除：在 App 內刪除影片會刪除 App 內的副本；已另外儲存到「照片」的影片不受影響。

如果未來版本新增雲端服務、第三方分析或其他資料處理方式，本政策與 App Store 隱私資訊會同步更新。

English

PromptCam requires no account and includes no advertising, user tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or third-party AI service.

Data on your device: Scripts, settings, recordings, thumbnails, and videos created by Quick Trim remain on your device or in locations you explicitly choose. The developer does not operate a server that receives this content.

Speech recognition: Voice Follow uses Apple Speech and prefers on-device recognition. If on-device recognition is unavailable for the current device or language, Apple speech services may require network processing. The app does not send your recordings or scripts to a developer-operated server.

Camera and microphone: Used only when recording or Voice Follow needs them. The app does not secretly record in the background.

Photos: Add-to-Photos access is requested only when you explicitly save a finished video.

Video trimming: Quick Trim is processed on the device. A new trimmed video is created while the original recording is kept; videos are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Files and sharing: Imported documents are used only to create scripts, and the share extension processes only content you explicitly share to PromptCam.

Deletion: Deleting a video in the app removes the app copy. Copies already saved to Photos are unaffected.

If a future release adds cloud services, third-party analytics, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated.
